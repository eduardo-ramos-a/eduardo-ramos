Valores

Quality mindset

Quality is everyone's job, not just a final check. I test early and often, so bugs get caught when they are cheap to fix.

Product and user first

I test like a real user, not just against the spec. A feature that passes every check but confuses people still counts as a bug.

Ownership

My archivology background taught me to care for information from creation to storage. In QA, I apply that by owning quality end to end, from test design to clear, traceable results.

Automation

If I do something twice, I automate it. I build UI and API tests with Playwright and REST Assured so teams get fast, reliable feedback.

Security

I check auth, input validation, and data exposure as part of regular testing. No secrets in repos, and least access by default.

Simple solutions first

I pick the simplest approach that solves the problem. I add complexity only when real needs show up, not before.
